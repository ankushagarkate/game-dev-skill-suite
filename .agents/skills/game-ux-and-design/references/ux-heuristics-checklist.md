# Game UX Heuristics & Playtest Checklist

## Pre-Playtest Usability Audit

| Pillar | Verification Questions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Signs & Feedback** | Does every button press, hit, pickup, and failure trigger clear audio/visual feedback within <100ms? |
| **Clarity** | Can a new player distinguish foreground interactables from background art without tooltips? |
| **Form Follows Function** | Do doors, ledges, destructibles, and hazards visually telegraph their behavior? |
| **Consistency** | Do confirm/cancel buttons, color semantics (red=danger, green=safe), and icons stay identical across all screens? |
| **Minimum Workload** | Are frequent actions 1–2 inputs away? Are tutorials broken into bite-sized playable moments? |
| **Error Prevention** | Are quit/delete/overwrite actions gated by confirmation? Can players recover from non-lethal mistakes? |
| **Accessibility** | Are subtitles, volume sliders, control remapping, and colorblind-safe indicators present? |

## Engage-ability & Flow Audit

- **Competence**: Does the player feel smarter/stronger after 5 minutes than at minute 1?
- **Autonomy**: Are there at least two viable ways to approach an encounter or build?
- **Relatedness**: If multiplayer/social, are ping/communication tools low-friction and toxicity-resistant?
- **Game Flow**: Plot tension vs. time—ensure high-intensity spikes are followed by short breather beats.

## Scientific UX Testing Protocol

1. **Define Hypothesis**: e.g., "Players will understand the stamina dodge mechanic within 30 seconds without text."
2. **Observe Without Leading**: Ask the tester to think aloud; do not rescue them immediately when stuck.
3. **Cross-Reference Telemetry**: Pair heatmaps/drop-off events with session recordings to separate *what happened* from *why*.
4. **Prioritize Fixes**: Fix blocking usability issues before tuning late-game balance.
